=== AUDIO FILE 1 — Slide 1 ===
Meet CRMITdesk Evolved. It's a single platform that brings your sales pipeline and your IT help desk together, with AI woven into almost every screen instead of bolted on as an afterthought.

=== AUDIO FILE 2 — Slide 2 ===
Most growing businesses end up running a CRM and a help desk as two completely separate tools. That means two logins, two databases, and no shared view of the customer — support can't see the deal that was just signed, and sales has no idea a customer has three open tickets. Worse, every integration, automation, and report has to be built and maintained twice.

=== AUDIO FILE 3 — Slide 3 ===
CRMITdesk Evolved fixes that by running sales and support side by side — same contacts, same organization, same login. On top of that shared foundation, we've layered AI across the whole platform, and a no-code automation engine that connects the two sides without needing a developer.

=== AUDIO FILE 4 — Slide 4 ===
Everything we're about to show you falls into three pillars: an all-in-one platform for CRM and IT support, AI built into nearly every screen, and no-code automation that connects it all together.

=== AUDIO FILE 5 — Slides 5-18 ===
Let's start with the first pillar: the all-in-one platform itself.

On the sales side, teams capture leads, qualify them, and convert them into deals in one click. A drag-and-drop board moves deals across your pipeline stages, quotes go out with proper line items, and campaigns reach your whole contact base. Built-in forecasting shows funnel value, win rate, and a probability-weighted revenue forecast — no spreadsheet required.

On the support side, tickets carry categories and priorities, and SLA deadlines are calculated automatically the moment a ticket is created. A knowledge base, an IT asset inventory, and ITIL-style change management round out the help desk — and every resolved ticket automatically triggers a customer satisfaction survey.

Email and WhatsApp conversations both land in one shared inbox, updating live as messages arrive. Agents filter by channel or status and always know who's already handling a conversation — so nothing falls through the cracks between channels.

And your customers get their own lightweight, branded portal. They log in with a secure, passwordless magic link, submit and track their own tickets, and see your logo and colors instead of ours — so it feels like part of your product, not a bolted-on tool.

The second pillar is AI — and this is where CRMITdesk Evolved really stands apart.

For sales, leads are scored automatically with a plain-language reason attached to every score. AI drafts follow-up emails and full nurture sequences, predicts win probability and likely close dates from your own pipeline history, and flags churn risk right on the contact page — so reps spend their time on the deals that matter.

For support, agents get suggested replies grounded in your own knowledge base, automatic routing suggestions, and duplicate detection that flags a redundant ticket before it's even submitted. Sentiment detection and SLA-risk scoring highlight which open tickets need attention first.

AI Studio is where the platform adapts to you. Describe your industry once, and it proposes your own terminology throughout the app — a ticket can become a 'case', a deal can become a 'policy', whatever fits your business — plus a starter set of automation rules. Nothing is applied until you've reviewed it and clicked Apply, so you're always the one in control.

And it's not limited to one screen. A natural-language command bar turns a typed instruction into a finished record. You can ask your dashboard a plain-English question and get a real, data-grounded answer back. You can even paste in raw meeting notes and get structured contacts, leads, and next steps out the other side.

The third pillar is automation — turning repetitive manual steps into rules that run themselves.

Managers build if-this-then-that automation rules across tickets, leads, and deals with no code at all — assign, re-prioritize, send an email, or message someone on WhatsApp, automatically, the moment something happens. Every rule keeps a running log so you can see exactly what fired and when.

Any staff member can schedule a WhatsApp reminder directly from a ticket or a deal — a one-off nudge or a recurring one — sent to the customer, the assignee, or any number you choose. Set it once, and the platform handles the rest in the background.

It also connects outward — Slack and Microsoft Teams get notified the moment a ticket comes in, an SLA breaches, or a deal is won. Outgoing webhooks are cryptographically signed so your systems can verify they really came from us, and a scoped API key gives developers programmatic access when they need it.

=== AUDIO FILE 6 — Slides 19-23 ===
None of this comes at the expense of security. Every organization's data is fully isolated, six-role access control governs who can see and do what, two-factor authentication and encrypted secrets protect your accounts and credentials, and a complete audit trail records every meaningful change.

Reporting rounds it out — live dashboards for pipeline, ticket volume, and SLA compliance, a short-term forecast for ticket volume, a probability-weighted revenue forecast, and one-click CSV export whenever you need the raw numbers.

And it's simple to get started. Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans all run the full platform — CRM, IT desk, and AI — with self-serve billing and plan management built right into the app, and seat-based pricing that scales as your team grows.

Stop switching between tools that don't talk to each other. CRMITdesk Evolved brings your sales team, your support team, and AI together in one place, so every conversation with a customer has the full picture behind it.
